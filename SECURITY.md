# Security Policy

## Supported scope

Security reports are accepted for the current hosted Lumina service and the
latest released version of the open-source project. Operators of modified or
older self-hosted installations are responsible for their deployment,
configuration, updates, and incident response.

## Report a vulnerability privately

Email **info@lumina-study.com** with the subject **Security report**. Do not
publish an exploitable weakness in Jira, GitHub Issues, or another public forum.

Please include:

- the affected hosted URL or Lumina version and deployment type;
- clear reproduction steps and the observed security impact;
- relevant logs, screenshots, or proof-of-concept material with secrets and
  unrelated personal data removed; and
- a safe way to contact you for follow-up.

Keep the issue private until a fix or mitigation is available and the project
has had reasonable time to investigate. Avoid privacy violations, destruction
of data, service disruption, social engineering, high-volume automated testing,
and accessing more data than needed to demonstrate the issue.

The project will acknowledge useful reports when practical and coordinate
remediation and disclosure. This version of the policy does not grant legal
safe harbour; any such language requires explicit legal and security approval.

Effective: 12 September 2026
